HOW TO PLAY

Setup

  1. Print one card per guest (blank cards are included for extra guests).
  2. Cut out the calling cards and put them in a hat or bowl.
  3. Hand out cards and markers – coins, candy, confetti or a pen.

Two ways to play

  • Live bingo: mark a square when it really happens at the party.
  • Classic bingo: the host draws calling cards and reads them out loud.
